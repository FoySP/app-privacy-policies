Privacy Policy

App: The Ravenscombe Mysteries (Android package com.steve.ravenscombe)
Effective date: 14 November 2026
Developer: the independent developer of The Ravenscombe Mysteries ("we", "us")

The Ravenscombe Mysteries is a single-player hidden-object puzzle and mystery game. This policy explains what information the game and its third-party services handle. In short: there are no accounts, we run no servers, and we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (episodes completed, clues found, hints used, settings and purchase entitlements) is saved in the game's private storage on your device. We do not receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game may show ads provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Approximate location (derived from your IP address)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device or other identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (such as app launch time and performance data)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is sent to Google and is encrypted in transit. It is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the game's settings where offered. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Buying the "remove ads" purchase stops ads from being shown.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (such as removing ads or unlocking extra episodes) are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. We only learn, through Google Play, that a purchase has been made so that we can unlock the content on your device. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics and crash reporting

The game does not currently send its own analytics or crash reports to us. If this changes, we will only collect anonymous usage events (for example, which levels are completed) that are not linked to your identity, and we will update this policy first.

5. Children

The game is a general-audience mystery game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game's advertising services, contact us (see below) and we will help.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. The only sharing is the advertising data described in section 2, which is collected by Google's SDK, not by us.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is no account to delete. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device. Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or California law) you may have rights over data held about you by Google; you can exercise them through your Google Account and Android settings. You can also contact us with any question.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at github.com/FoySP/app-privacy-policies/issues.
